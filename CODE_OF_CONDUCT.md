# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a fantasy football tool; nothing here is worth being unpleasant to
someone over.

## Expected

- Assume good faith, including from people whose rankings you think are wrong.
- Accept that the model is opinionated and reasonable people disagree with it.
- Give useful bug reports, and be patient with people who don't yet know how.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, or persistent disruption.

## Enforcement

Report to the repository owner through a private security advisory or direct contact.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions
that breach this, and may ban repeat offenders.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
